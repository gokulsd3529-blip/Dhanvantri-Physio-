Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided rehabilitation following appropriate surgery.

After surgery, guided exercise helps restore movement safely. Your surgeon's instructions always come first.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Recovery planning that follows your surgeon's advice
  • Gradual mobility restoration
  • Supervised strengthening exercises
  • Guidance on safe movement at home

Please bring discharge notes or your surgeon's instructions to the first visit.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
