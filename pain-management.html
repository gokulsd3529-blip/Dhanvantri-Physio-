Pain Management & Rehabilitation

Support for managing common muscular and joint pain.

Pain can limit work, sleep and daily activity. Rehabilitation looks at how pain affects your movement and builds a personalized approach.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Pain assessment and history
  • Functional movement support
  • Exercise and mobility guidance
  • Education on pacing and self-management

Outcomes differ from person to person, and no particular result can be guaranteed.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
