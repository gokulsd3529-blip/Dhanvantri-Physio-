Geriatric Physiotherapy

Mobility, balance and strength support for older adults.

Age-friendly physiotherapy helps older adults stay mobile, steady and independent, at a comfortable pace.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Mobility and balance support
  • Gentle strength and flexibility exercises
  • Fall-prevention education
  • Comfort-focused, well-paced sessions

Family members are welcome to join sessions to learn how to support exercises at home.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
