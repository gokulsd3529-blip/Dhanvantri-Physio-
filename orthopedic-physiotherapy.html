Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for joint, bone and muscle conditions.

Orthopedic physiotherapy supports people with problems of the joints, bones, muscles and ligaments. Care starts with an assessment of how the problem affects everyday movement.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Joint pain, stiffness and reduced mobility
  • Muscle and ligament problems
  • Recovery after fractures or orthopedic procedures, as advised by your doctor
  • Mobility, flexibility and strength exercises

Your plan is adjusted as your movement improves, and your therapist explains what you can do at home between visits.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
