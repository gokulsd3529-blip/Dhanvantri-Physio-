Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Structured recovery for sports and activity-related injuries.

Whether an injury happened during sport, exercise or daily activity, rehabilitation focuses on restoring movement and rebuilding strength gradually.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Assessment of the injury and movement limits
  • Mobility and strength training
  • Guided progression back to activity
  • Education on reducing the chance of repeat strain

Return-to-activity timing depends on the injury and the individual, and is agreed with you step by step.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
