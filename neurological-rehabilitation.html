Neurological Rehabilitation

Movement, balance and functional recovery support.

Neurological rehabilitation supports people whose movement or balance has been affected by a neurological condition, including after a stroke.

How physiotherapy may help

  • Movement and functional rehabilitation
  • Balance and coordination exercises
  • Stroke rehabilitation focused on independence
  • Guidance for family members and caregivers

Progress varies widely between individuals, so goals are set together and reviewed regularly.

Treatment is personalized and results cannot be guaranteed.
